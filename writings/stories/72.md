Gorgeous! – perfect, the way it radiates. A luminous gem, swearing allegiance to its setting. "I could have you. You will be mine." Carefully approaching, a whisper of intent. Brilliant! – it gleams and glows upon my touch. Fingers tracing boundaries, coaxing it free. Press it. Rub it. Tempted to yield its rightful place, but not quite. Then, a shift, a clutch – that's it! Turn and pull it. Yes – closer! Wrest it from its shell. At last! Yet, as my lips meet it, the victory crumbles. The ease of betrayal leaves it hollow.
