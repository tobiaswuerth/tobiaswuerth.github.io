When you suffer willingly to foster your growth,
when you prove your strength by allowing for weakness,
when you seek to enlighten the shadows within,
when you fight and struggle to become more righteous,
when you stride upward against all odds,
when you forgive and love in hope of redemption,
when you feel content by finding yourself,
but others deny and reject out of fear of change,
but others disregard the efforts they cannot muster,
but others misjudge and disrespect to aggrandize themselves,
but others blame and hate to favor their ignorance,
but others distance themselves due to shame,
but you need not care,
because they do not either,
you do you, because you want to,
because you can, because you are,
because you allowed yourself to become,
in spite of others,
for yourself,
truly.
