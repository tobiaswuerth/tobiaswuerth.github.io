“No one is perfect”, they say, and “You’re perfect just the way you are”. How paradoxical: Being imperfect is being perfect? Then again, who’s to judge? You might deem my flaws perfect or my perfection flawed. Yet your judgment ought to be irrelevant to how I see myself. Otherwise, I would deform myself in a futile attempt to match foreign standards. I must therefore be the sole steward of my measures. But how can I bear my inadequacies without constantly fighting myself? The mind cannot fully fathom contradictions. One way to properly grasp a paradox is to do so beyond the mind, through unconscious embodiment. This means living my imperfection, allowing myself to learn and grow, while also living my perfection, accepting and loving myself fully. Tolerance without stagnation. Being while becoming. To encompass and unite both light and shadow on the journey of self-actualization.
