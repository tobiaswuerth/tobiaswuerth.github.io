You lay your eyes upon debris
Fallen structures and split trees
But the only thing you see
Is the vision of what it could be
So you take a knee and free a piece
Brush the dust off and clean it deep
Hold it dear and call it thine
Let the sun reign and make it shine
Love it fully in heart and mind
Your essence in vein and gist of life
For nature's course it will revive
Upon a new day and touch the skies
