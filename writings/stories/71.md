When pain and sorrow flood the heart
for the loss of what could have been,
one is harshly reminded
that our worlds are rich with meaning.
For if it did not hurt,
it never truly mattered.
And it is not by choice
but by the nature that binds us
to cherish what we deem valuable.
For better or worse,
we are destined to care.
How ironic, then,
that we are forced to live meaningful lives
— to find salvation in the burden.
